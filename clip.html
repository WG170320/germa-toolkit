Questions

Good to know

How are views counted?

We read the public view count on your submitted TikTok link and update your balance as it climbs. Only TikTok is supported right now.

How do I get paid?

Cash out three ways: MLBB Diamonds to your Game ID (min 5), Robux to your Roblox username (min 100), or GCash to your number (min ₱50). 1 Diamond = ₱1 = 1 Robux.

Can I post more than one clip?

Yes. Submit as many original TikToks as you want - every qualifying clip adds to your total.

What gets a clip rejected?

Bought or bot views, re-uploading someone else's video, or clips that aren't actually about Germa Toolkit.
